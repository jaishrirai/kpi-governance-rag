=== RUN A ===
[A G-018] Q: How many days do we use to count an Active Customer?
A: We use a 30-day window to count an Active Customer, which is the only certified definition [KPI-006::v1::0]. An informal team FAQ mentions a 90-day window, but that source is provisional and not authoritative [FAQ-01::v1::0].

[A G-013] Q: Customer churn rate - how is that calculated? What dashboards use it?
A: Customer Churn Rate (KPI-007) is calculated using the formula: Churn Rate % = Customers in the start base with no purchase for 180 days by quarter end / Customers in the start base x 100, where the start base is every identified customer with at least one completed order in the 180 days before the quarter starts [KPI-007::v1::0]. The dashboards that use Customer Churn Rate are the Customer 360 Dashboard and the Executive KPI Pack [KPI-007::v1::0]. (The older v1 version is deprecated and must not be used) [KPI-007::v1::0, KPI-007-V1::v1::0].

[A G-002] Q: What KPIs around customer churn which are enabled right now?
A: Customer Churn Rate v2 (KPI-007) is currently enabled and certified, defined as the share of recently active customers who stop buying after 180 days without a completed purchase [KPI-007::v1::0]. The previous version, KPI-007-V1, counted only account closures and is deprecated and not authoritative [KPI-007::v1::0, KPI-007-V1::v1::0].

[A G-017] Q: What is NRR and how is it different from churn?
A: Net Revenue Retention (NRR) measures how much net revenue Kestrel keeps from an existing group of customers over 12 months, including growth and losses from customers who stop buying, while excluding newly acquired customers [KPI-010::v1::0]. In contrast, Customer Churn Rate measures the share of recently active customers who stop buying from Kestrel, defined as 180 days passing without a completed purchase in any channel [KPI-007::v1::0].

[A G-001] Q: What are key KPIs and Dashboards serving Customer Domain. Give me strcutured asnwer.
A: The dashboard serving the Customer Domain is the Customer 360 Dashboard (CAT-02), which provides a single view of customer health including activity, churn, retention, lifetime value, repeat buying, and NPS, with linked KPIs KPI-006, KPI-007, KPI-010, KPI-011, KPI-012, and KPI-013 [CAT-02::v1::0]. Additionally, the customers table (CAT-08) in the Customer domain holds one row per identified customer containing loyalty tier, account status, first order date, acquisition channel, and staff and test flags, with linked KPIs KPI-006, KPI-007, KPI-010, KPI-011, KPI-012, and KPI-014 [CAT-08::v1::0].
