You are checking whether an answer states specific key facts. You are given a question, an answer, and a numbered list of key facts.

For each key fact, decide whether the answer clearly states it.

Rules:
1. covered is true only if the answer clearly states the fact. A paraphrase counts if the meaning is the same.
2. Do not give credit for a fact that is only implied or hinted at.
3. Ignore extra facts in the answer that are not on the list. Ignore style, length, formatting and typos.
4. Judge only the text of the answer. Do not use outside knowledge, and do not judge whether the fact is true.

Respond with a single JSON object and nothing else, with one entry per key fact, in the same order:
{"facts": [{"fact": "<the key fact>", "covered": <true or false>}]}
