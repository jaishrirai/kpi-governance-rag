# Marketing ROI (ROAS) (KPI-015)

## Definition
Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) is the net revenue generated by paid media for each unit of currency spent on it. It is the main marketing ROI measure at Kestrel.

## Formula
ROAS = Attributed net revenue / Paid media spend

Attributed net revenue is the Net Revenue (KPI-001) of app and web orders where the last paid click happened within 7 days before the order.

## Grain and filters
- Reported weekly and monthly.
- Split by channel and campaign.
- Only paid media spend is included; owned channels such as email are excluded.

## Source tables
- `marketing_spend`: campaign, channel, spend
- `sales_orders`: order id, net value, last paid click and click date

## Dashboards
- Marketing Effectiveness Dashboard

## Caveats and exclusions
- Provisional: the attribution method (last paid click versus a data-driven model) is still under review.
- Store sales influenced by online ads are not attributed, so ROAS understates total impact.
- Not approved for board reporting or the Executive KPI Pack.
